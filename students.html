Students

Welcome to Eskişehir Technical University. Below you can find useful resources for new and current students.

ESTU OYS
Online Learning System (OYS) is the digital learning platform used by Eskişehir Technical University to support teaching and learning activities. Through this system, students can access course materials, lecture notes, assignments, and announcements shared by their instructors.
OYS allows students to participate in online discussions, submit assignments, and follow course updates in a structured environment. It also enables instructors to manage course content efficiently and communicate with students outside the classroom. By using OYS, students can continue their learning process anytime and anywhere.

Go to ESTU OYS
ESTU OBS
Student Information System (OBS) is the official platform used by students to manage their academic records and administrative processes. Through OBS, students can register for courses, view their grades, track their academic progress, and access important announcements related to their education.
The system also provides access to personal academic information such as transcripts, course schedules, and exam results. OBS plays an essential role in helping students organize their academic life and stay informed about university procedures.

Go to ESTU OBS
ESTU MOBILE APP
ESTU Mobile Application is the official mobile app developed for Eskişehir Technical University students and staff. The application allows users to access important university services directly from their smartphones.
Through the mobile application, students can follow announcements, check academic information, access campus services, and stay updated about university events. The ESTU Mobile Application provides a fast and convenient way for students to stay connected with university life anytime and anywhere

Go to download app
TURNA PORTAL
Turna Portal is the institutional portal system used by Eskişehir Technical University to provide integrated access to various university services. Through this portal, students can reach multiple academic and administrative systems from a single platform without needing separate logins.
The portal helps students easily navigate university resources such as announcements, academic systems, and internal services. Turna Portal improves accessibility and communication between students and the university by bringing different digital services together in one centralized interface.
